# Code of conduct

CarbonHV adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and
keep discussions on topic. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing other
people's private information are not tolerated.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/itsrhotaudevvy/CarbonHV/security/advisories/new)
(choose "conduct" in the title) or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's community impact
guidelines.
